* Course description
The definite integral, techniques of integration, and applications to physics,
chemistry, and engineering. Sequences, series, and power series. Taylor and
Maclaurin series.

MATH 132 is a four-credit General Education course that satisfies the *R1*
(Basic Math Skills) and *R2* (Analytic Reasoning) requirements for graduation.

** Prerequisite
*MATH 131* or equivalent.

** Textbook
+ ~[S]~ J. Stewart, /Calculus: Early Transcendentals/, 9th edition.

Enhanced [[https://www.webassign.net/][WebAssign]] (access code + /e/-book) is *required*: your homework is
done there. The /e/-book and the access code are bundled together inside your
Canvas course, so *a physical copy of the textbook is not required*.

** Learning objectives
By the end of this course, you will be able to:
1. Apply integration techniques to evaluate definite and indefinite integrals.
2. Use integration in applications such as computing areas, volumes, and arc
   lengths.
3. Represent functions as power series, and find Taylor and Maclaurin series.
4. Analyze curves and solve problems using parametric equations and polar
   coordinates.
