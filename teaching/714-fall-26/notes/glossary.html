Glossary

Glossary

affine \(k\)-variety
A scheme over \(k\) isomorphic to \(\Spec A\) for some \(k\)-algebra \(A\) of finite type. Chapter 2
affine \(n\)-space
The \(k\)-variety \(\AA^n_k = \Spec k[x_1, \dots, x_n]\), with \(\AA^n(R) = R^n\) for every \(k\)-algebra \(R\). Chapter 2
\(b\)-invariants
The polynomials \(b_2, b_4, b_6, b_8\) in the coefficients of a Weierstrass equation, from which \(\Delta\) is built. Chapter 4
base extension
For a \(k\)-variety \(X\) and a field \(L \supseteq k\), the \(L\)-variety \(X_L = X \times_{\Spec k} \Spec L\), defined by the same equations as \(X\). Chapter 2
\(c\)-invariants
The polynomials \(c_4 = b_2^2 - 24b_4\) and \(c_6 = -b_2^3 + 36b_2b_4 - 216b_6\) of a Weierstrass equation; they satisfy \(1728\Delta = c_4^3 - c_6^2\). Chapter 4
canonical divisor
The divisor of a non-zero differential on a nice curve. Chapter 3
chart
A homeomorphism from an open subset of a topological space onto an open subset of \(\C\). Chapter 1
closed point
A point \(P\) of a \(k\)-variety whose residue field \(k(P)\) is finite over \(k\) (for varieties, equivalently \(\{P\}\) is closed); its degree is \([k(P) : k]\). Chapter 3
compatible charts
Two charts whose domains are disjoint, or whose transition maps are holomorphic. Chapter 1
complex structure
A family of pairwise compatible charts whose domains cover the space. Chapter 1
complex torus
The quotient \(\C/\Lambda\) of \(\C\) by a lattice \(\Lambda\): a compact Riemann surface that is also an abelian group. Chapter 1
connected
A topological space that is not the union of two disjoint non-empty open subsets. Chapter 1
cubic twist
For \(d \in k^\times\), the Selmer curve \(C_d\), regarded as a twist of the Fermat cubic \(C_1\); its isomorphism class depends only on \(d\) in \(k^\times/k^{\times 3}\). Chapter 4
curve
An integral variety of dimension \(1\). Chapter 3
cyclic isogeny
An isogeny of complex tori whose kernel is a cyclic group. Chapter 1
definite quaternion algebra
A quaternion algebra \(B\) over \(\Q\) with \(B \otimes_\Q \R\) isomorphic to Hamilton's quaternions; \((a, b \mid \Q)\) is definite exactly when \(a, b < 0\). Chapter 4
degree of a morphism
For a non-constant morphism of curves, the degree \([k(C_1) : \varphi^* k(C_2)]\) of the extension of function fields. Chapter 3
degree of an isogeny of complex tori
The order of its kernel; for \(z \mapsto \alpha z\) from \(\C/\Lambda_1\) to \(\C/\Lambda_2\) it is the index \([\Lambda_2 : \alpha\Lambda_1]\). Chapter 1
differential
An element of \(\Omega_{k(C)/k}\), the \(k(C)\)-vector space generated by the \(dx\) subject to the Leibniz rules and \(da = 0\) for \(a \in k\). Chapter 3
discriminant
The polynomial \(\Delta\) in the coefficients of a Weierstrass equation that vanishes exactly when the cubic is singular; \(\Delta = -16(4A^3 + 27B^2)\) for \(y^2 = x^3 + Ax + B\). Chapter 4
divisor
A finite formal integer combination of closed points of a curve. Chapter 3
dual isogeny
For an isogeny \(\varphi\) of degree \(d\), the unique isogeny \(\hat\varphi\) with \(\hat\varphi \circ \varphi = [d]\). Chapter 4
dual isogeny of complex tori
For an isogeny \(z \mapsto \alpha z\) of complex tori of degree \(d\), the isogeny \(z \mapsto (d/\alpha)z\) in the opposite direction; both composites are multiplication by \(d\). Chapter 1
Eisenstein integers
The ring \(\Z[\omega]\), where \(\omega\) is a primitive cube root of \(1\). Chapter 1
Eisenstein series
For a lattice \(\Lambda\) and even \(k > 2\), the sum \(G_k(\Lambda)\) of \(\omega^{-k}\) over the non-zero \(\omega \in \Lambda\). Chapter 1
elliptic curve
A pair \((E, O)\) of a nice curve \(E\) of genus \(1\) over \(k\) and a rational point \(O \in E(k)\). Chapter 4
finite type
A scheme over \(k\) covered by finitely many open subschemes \(\Spec A\), each with \(A\) a finitely generated \(k\)-algebra. Chapter 2
free
An abelian group that has a \(\Z\)-basis. Chapter 1
function field
For an integral variety \(X\), the local ring \(k(X)\) at its generic point, which is a field; for \(X = \Spec A\) it is \(\Frac A\). Chapter 2
fundamental group
The group \(\pi_1(X, x)\) of homotopy classes of loops in \(X\) based at \(x\). Chapter 1
fundamental parallelogram
For a lattice with \(\Z\)-basis \(\{\omega_1, \omega_2\}\), the half-open parallelogram \(\{t_1\omega_1 + t_2\omega_2 : 0 \leq t_1, t_2 < 1\}\). Chapter 1
Gaussian integers
The ring \(\Z[i]\). Chapter 1
genus
For a nice curve, \(\ell(K_C)\), the dimension of the space of regular differentials. Chapter 3
geometrically integral
A \(k\)-variety \(X\) such that \(X_{\kbar}\) is integral. Chapter 2
Hausdorff
A topological space in which any two distinct points have disjoint open neighborhoods. Chapter 1
holomorphic differential
A meromorphic differential on a Riemann surface whose local expressions \(f_\phi(z)\,dz\) are all holomorphic. Chapter 1
holomorphic map
A continuous map between Riemann surfaces that is holomorphic when read in charts. Chapter 1
homogenization
The homogeneous polynomial \(F(X_0, \dots, X_n) = X_0^{\deg f} f(X_1/X_0, \dots, X_n/X_0)\) attached to \(f \in k[x_1, \dots, x_n]\). Chapter 2
integral variety
A \(k\)-variety whose rings of sections over non-empty opens are integral domains; equivalently, a reduced and irreducible one. Chapter 2
intersection number
For plane curves \(C = V_+(F)\) and \(D = V_+(G)\) without common component, \(I_P(C, D) = \dim_k \calO_{\PP^2_k,P}/(f, g)\). Chapter 3
invariant differential
The differential \(\omega = dx/(2y + a_1x + a_3)\) of a Weierstrass equation; it has no zeros or poles and is invariant under translation. Chapter 4
invertible module
A finitely generated projective module of constant rank \(1\) over a ring \(R\); equivalently, an \(R\)-module \(P\) with \(P \otimes_R Q \cong R\) for some \(Q\). Chapter 2
irreducible variety
A \(k\)-variety whose underlying topological space is non-empty and not the union of two proper closed subsets. Chapter 2
isogeny
A non-zero holomorphic group homomorphism between complex tori. Its algebraic analogue is an isogeny of elliptic curves. Chapter 1
isogeny of elliptic curves
A morphism \(\varphi\colon E_1 \to E_2\) of elliptic curves with \(\varphi(O_1) = O_2\); it is automatically a group homomorphism. Chapter 4
isomorphism of complex tori
An invertible isogeny. Chapter 1
isomorphism of Riemann surfaces
A bijective holomorphic map whose inverse is holomorphic. Chapter 1
\(j\)-invariant
The quantity \(j = c_4^3/\Delta\) of a smooth Weierstrass equation; it classifies elliptic curves up to isomorphism over \(\kbar\). Chapter 4
\(k\)-variety
A separated scheme of finite type over a field \(k\). Chapter 2
lattice
A subgroup of \(\C\), free of rank 2, that spans \(\C\) as an \(\R\)-vector space. Chapter 1
lattice in an algebra
A finitely generated \(\Z\)-submodule of a finite-dimensional \(\Q\)-algebra \(B\) that spans \(B\) over \(\Q\). Chapter 4
line at infinity
The line \(Z = 0\) of \(\PP^2_k\), the complement of the affine patch \(Z \neq 0\). Chapter 4
\(m\)-torsion subgroup
The kernel \((\C/\Lambda)[m]\) of multiplication by \(m\); it is isomorphic to \((\Z/m\Z)^2\). Chapter 1
maximal order
An order not properly contained in another order. Chapter 4
meromorphic differential on a Riemann surface
A family of expressions \(f_\phi(z)\,dz\), one for each chart \(\phi\), with \(f_\phi\) meromorphic, related on overlaps by the chain rule. Chapter 1
meromorphic function
A holomorphic map from a Riemann surface to \(\PP^1(\C)\) that is not identically \(\infty\). Chapter 1
multiplication by \(m\) map
The isogeny \([m]\colon \C/\Lambda \to \C/\Lambda\), \(z + \Lambda \mapsto mz + \Lambda\). Chapter 1
multiplicative group
The \(k\)-variety \(\Gm = \Spec k[x, x^{-1}]\), with \(\Gm(R) = R^\times\) for every \(k\)-algebra \(R\). Chapter 2
nice variety
A smooth, projective, geometrically integral variety. Chapter 2
nonsingular polynomial
A homogeneous \(F \in \C[Z_0, Z_1, Z_2]\) such that \(F\) and its three partial derivatives have no common zero in \(\PP^2(\C)\). Chapter 1
open unit disk
The Riemann surface \(\mathbb{D} = \{z \in \C : \lvert z \rvert < 1\}\). Chapter 1
order
A lattice in a finite-dimensional \(\Q\)-algebra that is also a subring. Chapter 4
Picard group
The group \(\Pic C\) of divisors on a curve \(C\) modulo principal divisors. Chapter 3
point at infinity
The rational point \(O = (0 : 1 : 0)\), the only point of a Weierstrass cubic on the line at infinity, where it is a flex. Chapter 4
principal divisor
The divisor \(\div(f) = \sum_P \ord_P(f) P\) of a non-zero rational function. Chapter 3
projective closure
The Zariski closure in \(\PP^n_k\) of a closed subvariety \(X = V(I)\) of \(\AA^n_k = U_0\); it is cut out by the homogeneous ideal generated by the homogenizations of all elements of \(I\). Chapter 2
projective plane
The space \(\PP^2(\C) = \bigl(\C^3 - \{(0,0,0)\}\bigr)/\C^\times\), with points written \((Z_0 : Z_1 : Z_2)\). Chapter 1
projective plane curve
The zero locus \(V_+(F) \subseteq \PP^2_k\) of a non-constant homogeneous polynomial with no repeated factor over \(\kbar\). Chapter 3
quadratic twist
For \(d \in k^\times\) and \(\operatorname{char} k \neq 2\), the curve \(E^d\colon y^2 = x^3 + d^2Ax + d^3B\) attached to \(E\colon y^2 = x^3 + Ax + B\); it has the same \(j\)-invariant, and its isomorphism class depends only on \(d\) in \(k^\times/k^{\times 2}\). Chapter 4
quaternion algebra
An algebra over a field \(F\) with basis \(1, i, j, ij\) and \(i^2 = a\), \(j^2 = b\), \(ji = -ij\) for some \(a, b \in F^\times\); written \((a, b \mid F)\). Chapter 4
ramification divisor
For a separable morphism \(\varphi\), the divisor \(\div(\varphi^*\omega) - \varphi^*\div(\omega)\). Chapter 3
ramification index
For a non-constant morphism of curves, \(e_\varphi(P) = \ord_P(\varphi^* t)\) with \(t\) a uniformizer at \(\varphi(P)\). Chapter 3
rank
The number of elements of a \(\Z\)-basis of a free abelian group. Chapter 1
rational map
For an integral variety \(X\), a morphism to \(Y\) from a non-empty open subvariety of \(X\), up to agreement on overlaps. Chapter 2
rational point
A point of a \(k\)-variety \(X\) with values in \(k\), i.e. an element of \(X(k) = \Hom_k(\Spec k, X)\). Chapter 2
reduced norm
The element \(\nrd(\alpha) = \alpha\bar\alpha\) of \(F\), for \(\alpha\) in a quaternion algebra over \(F\); it is multiplicative. Chapter 4
reduced trace
The element \(\trd(\alpha) = \alpha + \bar\alpha\) of \(F\), for \(\alpha\) in a quaternion algebra over \(F\). Chapter 4
reduced variety
A \(k\)-variety whose rings of sections have no non-zero nilpotents. Chapter 2
regular differential
A differential \(\omega\) that is zero or has \(\div(\omega) \geq 0\); these form a \(k\)-vector space. Chapter 3
regular variety
A variety whose local rings are all regular local rings. Chapter 2
relative Frobenius morphism
For \(C \subseteq \PP^n_k\) with \(q\) a power of \(\operatorname{char} k > 0\), the morphism \(C \to C^{(q)}\) raising homogeneous coordinates to the \(q\)-th power, where \(C^{(q)}\) is defined by the equations of \(C\) with coefficients raised to the \(q\)-th power. Chapter 3
Riemann sphere
The projective line \(\PP^1(\C)\), a compact Riemann surface covered by two charts. Chapter 1
Riemann surface
A connected Hausdorff topological space with a complex structure. Chapter 1
scheme
A locally ringed space covered by open subsets isomorphic to affine schemes \(\Spec A\). Chapter 2
second countable
A topological space with a countable basis for its topology. Chapter 1
Selmer elliptic curve
For \(d \in k^\times\) and \(\operatorname{char} k \neq 3\), the plane cubic \(C_d\colon X^3 + Y^3 = dZ^3\) with base point \(O = (1 : -1 : 0)\). Chapter 4
separated
A scheme \(X\) over \(S\) whose diagonal \(X \to X \times_S X\) is a closed immersion. Chapter 2
simply connected
A connected topological space whose fundamental group is trivial. Chapter 1
smooth projective plane curve
The zero locus in \(\PP^2(\C)\) of a nonsingular homogeneous polynomial. Chapter 1
smooth variety
A variety which, locally cut out of \(\AA^n_k\) with pure dimension \(d\), has Jacobian matrix of rank \(n - d\) at every \(\kbar\)-point; equivalently, \(X_{\kbar}\) is regular. Chapter 2
split quaternion algebra
A quaternion algebra over \(F\) isomorphic to \(M_2(F)\); equivalently, one that is not a division ring. Chapter 4
standard involution
On a quaternion algebra, the conjugation \(t + xi + yj + zij \mapsto t - xi - yj - zij\); in general, an \(F\)-linear anti-automorphism of order at most \(2\) with \(\alpha\bar\alpha \in F\) for all \(\alpha\). Chapter 4
Tate module
For a prime \(\ell \neq \operatorname{char} k\), the \(\Z_\ell\)-module \(T_\ell(E) = \varprojlim_n E[\ell^n](\ksep)\), free of rank \(2\). Chapter 4
transition map
For charts \(\phi_1\) and \(\phi_2\), the map \(\phi_2 \circ \phi_1^{-1}\colon \phi_1(U_1 \cap U_2) \to \phi_2(U_1 \cap U_2)\). Chapter 1
translation-invariant differential
On \(\C^\times\) or a complex torus, a meromorphic differential \(\omega\) with \(\tau_a^*\omega = \omega\) for every translation \(\tau_a\). Chapter 1
twisted cubic
The affine curve \(V(x_2 - x_1^2, x_3 - x_1^3) \subseteq \AA^3_k\), the image of \(t \mapsto (t, t^2, t^3)\). Chapter 2
uniformizer
A function \(t\) with \(\ord_P(t) = 1\) at a regular closed point \(P\). Chapter 3
upper half plane
The Riemann surface \(\bbH = \{z \in \C : \Im(z) > 0\}\). Chapter 1
Weierstrass coordinates
Functions \(x, y\) on an elliptic curve with poles of order \(2\) and \(3\) at \(O\) only, giving an isomorphism onto a Weierstrass cubic. Chapter 4
Weierstrass equation
A projective plane cubic \(Y^2Z + a_1XYZ + a_3YZ^2 = X^3 + a_2X^2Z + a_4XZ^2 + a_6Z^3\). Chapter 4
Weierstrass \(\wp\)-function
For a lattice \(\Lambda\), the meromorphic function \(\wp_\Lambda(z) = z^{-2} + \sum_{\omega \in \Lambda - \{0\}} \bigl[(z - \omega)^{-2} - \omega^{-2}\bigr]\) on \(\C\). Chapter 1
Weil pairing
For \(N\) not divisible by \(\operatorname{char} k\), the bilinear, alternating, non-degenerate, Galois-equivariant pairing \(e_N\colon E[N](\ksep) \times E[N](\ksep) \to \mu_N(\ksep)\). Chapter 4
Weil reciprocity
For non-zero rational functions \(f\), \(g\) on a nice curve whose divisors have disjoint support, the identity \(f(\div g) = g(\div f)\), where \(f(D) = \prod_P f(P)^{n_P}\) for \(D = \sum n_P P\). Chapter 3
