Chapter 7Galois cohomology

This chapter follows [Sil09, Appendix B] and [Sil09, Section VIII.2]. For the point of view on twists, torsors and étale cohomology we follow [Poo17, Sections 1.3, 1.5, 4.4, 4.5, 5.12 and 6.2–6.6].

Situation 7.0.1.

  • \(K\) is a field, not assumed perfect, with a separable closure \(\Ksep\) and absolute Galois group \(\Gal_K \coloneqq \Gal(\Ksep/K)\).
  • For a discrete \(\Gal_K\)-module \(M\) we write \(H^i(K, M) \coloneqq H^i(\Gal_K, M)\). For a smooth commutative algebraic group \(G\) over \(K\), such as \(\Gm\) or \(E\), we write \(H^i(K, G) \coloneqq H^i(K, G(\Ksep))\).
  • \(E\) is an elliptic curve over \(K\).
  • \(m \geq 1\) is an integer and \(\mu_m \coloneqq \Gm[m]\). Unless stated otherwise, \(\operatorname{char} K \nmid m\). Then \(\mu_m(\Ksep) \cong \Z/m\Z\) and \(E[m](\Ksep) \cong (\Z/m\Z)^2\) as groups.
